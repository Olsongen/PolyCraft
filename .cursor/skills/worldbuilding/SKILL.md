# Worldbuilding

Read `docs/VISION.md` and the active kit brief. World comes **before** kit plan.

## Job

Make a place a lead would sign. If the paragraph could caption any dungeon, it failed.

## Must answer

- Who lived here, who is gone, what the space can still show.
- The event that made the ruin (or the living place). Prefer causality over spectacle.
- Player path at the named camera (this kit: FPS, 1.7 m).
- Scale: what one module means in this room.
- Landmarks vs set dressing.
- Kill list: what would be *wrong* here.

## Middlehelm wetlands (active)

Founding act is **drain**. Failure is **water returning**. Ref grammar is Trois-Fontaines (Cistercian bays, vaults failing as bays, trees in the nave). Do not import the 18th-century gateway. Temperate wetland overgrowth, not jungle.

## Output

Write into `world` + `killList` of the kit brief (`brief.json` and `BRIEF.md`). Do not emit a module table in this skill.
