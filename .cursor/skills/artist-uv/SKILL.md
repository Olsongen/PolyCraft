# Artist-grade UVs

PolyCraft solves UVs. PolyMesh may assist (decimation / learned insight). Stock Unwrap_UVW until it is the ceiling; then a custom solver is in charter.

## Kit policy

Read `uvStrategy` on the **active** brief. Do not apply another kit’s trim/waterline/hero unique set by default.

- **Trim / stack** modular islands when the brief says so.
- **Unique** only what the brief names as hero or broken.
- Texel locked across the material family the brief owns.
- UV2 only if that kit’s handoff names it.

## Solve bar

Seams serve the module. No 0-area islands. No random peel. Checker reads even at the briefed camera on the surfaces the player stares at.

## Output

Document deviations in the brief. If Unwrap cannot hit this bar on the kit, that is a plugin job — name the blockage, then build.
