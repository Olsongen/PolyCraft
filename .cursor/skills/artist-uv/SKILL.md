# Artist-grade UVs

PolyCraft solves UVs. PolyMesh may assist (decimation / learned insight). Stock Unwrap_UVW until it is the ceiling; then a custom solver is in charter.

## Kit policy (Middlehelm)

- **Trim sheet:** ashlar courses, 1.0 m waterline, arcade inner profile, pier wrap. All modular walls share it.
- **Stack** modular islands.
- **Unique:** west front, surviving vault, fallen bay slabs, nave tree.
- **Do not** unique-unwrap every 4 m arcade.
- Texel locked across the stone family.
- UV2 only if Source handoff is named.

## Solve bar

Seams serve the module. No 0-area islands. No random peel. Checker reads even at FPS eye on the first courses (wet stone is what the player stares at).

## Output

Document deviations in the brief. If Unwrap cannot hit this bar on the kit, that is a plugin job — name the blockage, then build.
